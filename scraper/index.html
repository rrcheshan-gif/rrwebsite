Engineering
Sri Lanka's

For three decades, RR Construction has turned ambitious blueprints into the roads, bridges, harbours, and water systems that keep Sri Lanka moving forward — built to last, delivered on time.

01 / HIGHWAY NETWORKS & INFRASTRUCTURE

Carving Nationwide
Expressway Systems

As a leading civil engineering partner, we construct key expressway packages across Sri Lanka. Our expertise in major excavation, stabilization, and asphalt laying accelerates connectivity.

02 / MARITIME & HARBOUR CONSTRUCTION

Building Sri Lanka's
Maritime Heritage

We deliver advanced maritime infrastructure, including dredging, breakwaters, and harbour facility development, ensuring robust connectivity for Sri Lanka’s coastal trade and economic growth.

03 / BRIDGES & OVERPASSES

Structural Integrity
Spanning Generations

Using high-strength concrete and advanced structural steel fabrication, we build river crossings, heavy overpasses, and causeways designed to carry extreme traffic loads safely.

04 / LANDSLIDE & SOIL MITIGATION

Stabilizing Communities
Under Geological Threat

In critical hillside zones (Nuwara Eliya, Hatton, Kandy), we deploy state-of-the-art geotechnical engineering. Soil retaining structures and rock-fall nets keep national roadways secure.

05 / AGGREGATES & MATERIAL PRODUCTION

Sustainable Aggregate
Supply Chain

We operate large-scale, eco-friendly M-Sand and crusher facilities to supply high-quality, continuous aggregates. Our self-reliant production ensures superior concrete strength for major infrastructure projects nationwide.

06 / HEAVY RESOURCE FLEET

Self-Owned Assets
For Speed & Independence

We operate a massive self-owned fleet of 1,000+ heavy machines and high-output asphalt plants. This independence ensures zero dependency on third-party schedules.

Sustainable Engineering

We recognize our responsibility to the environment. Through efficient resource management, sustainable material sourcing, and strict adherence to ISO 14001 Environmental Management Systems, we minimize our ecological footprint while building infrastructure that lasts.

Health & Safety First

Zero harm is our ultimate goal. We maintain a rigorous Health & Safety culture on every site, backed by our ISO 45001 certification. Our 1,800+ workforce is continuously trained to operate under the highest global safety standards, ensuring everyone goes home safely.

Our Legacy

THREE DECADES OF
EXCELLENCE

Since 1995, RR Construction has helped shape the physical foundations of modern Sri Lanka. What began as a subcontracting partnership with international firms like ED Zublin AG has grown into one of the country's most trusted construction names.

Hundreds of completed projects later — expressways, deep-water dredging, bridges spanning over 100 metres, and water treatment plants serving entire districts — our portfolio speaks for itself.
